# Week 15 Vocabulary Pictures

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this lesson. These are fictional educational reconstructions, not archival photographs. No named historical person's likeness was requested.

Shared specification: a precise 4x4 contact sheet, overall landscape aspect ratio 8:5; each of the 16 equal tiles is also landscape 8:5. Edge-to-edge pictures, no gutters, captions, labels, readable text, logos, or watermarks. Clear natural light, realistic Thai/Southeast Asian settings, 1940s period details where appropriate. Subjects completely inside their tile with breathing room. Humane neutral scenes without political insignia, propaganda, graphic violence, or glorification. Cropped row-major and saved at 640x400 pixels. The entire tile is retained; card images use contain rather than cover.

## Reproduce the Crops

Use the project's `scripts/crop_vocab_grid.py` with `--start 1`, `17`, or `33`. For the third grid use `--count 8`. Originals remain in the image tool's generated-images directory; WebP contact sheets and all used individual tiles are stored here.
